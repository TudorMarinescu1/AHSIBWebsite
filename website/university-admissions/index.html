IB Experiences

University Admissions

IB helps students develop the reading, writing, research, discussion, and time-management habits they will carry into college and beyond.

The value of an IB education

A strong academic foundation for what comes next

Through sustained coursework, independent research, and assessments across subjects, IB students practice the kind of analytical and communication skills expected in university classes.

Show your academic preparation

IB coursework can help students demonstrate that they pursued a rigorous, broad academic program while developing depth in their Higher Level subjects.

Plan credit thoughtfully

College credit, placement, and course equivalencies differ by university, major, exam level, and score. Review each college's current IB credit policy before making enrollment decisions.

Use your IB experience

Students can describe research, collaboration, service, language study, and academic growth in applications, interviews, and college essays.

College-credit questions

Check policies directly with each university

Universities set their own rules for IB credit. Some recognize a completed diploma, while others award credit or placement for qualifying Higher Level examinations. Policies can change, so use each institution's official admissions or registrar website for the most current information.

Planning tip

Keep a list of the IB subjects and levels you plan to complete, then compare it with the credit policies at the colleges you are considering.
