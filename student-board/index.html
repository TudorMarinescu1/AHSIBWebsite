IB Student Board

What is it

The IB Student Board is a student-run body designed to aid in the coordination of the IB Programme. Its job is to help the IB coordinator as well as the IB teachers in ensuring the success of IB students. The board will be able to maintain a tight structure between the key players of the IB Programme: teachers and students. Such unification is essential for the IB Programme at Agoura to remain as prosperous as ever. With this organization in place, IB students and parents will be more aware of what students are required to take. Knowing this information will hopefully both keep students passionate about the programme and allow parents to feel more secure about their child’s academic years in IB.
