For Students

Build a schedule you can actually live with

IB is rigorous, but it is also flexible. Students can pursue the full diploma, the AHS IB Merit Scholar path, or individual course certificates.

9th

Start foundations

Explore interests, take strong English, math, science, world language, PreIB, Honors, AP, or college-prep courses. Begin thinking about how these courses align with your future goals.

10th

Prepare for choices

Continue pursuing your interests, continue world language study, and look at which IB subjects fit your goals before junior-year schedules are due early in the second semester.

11th

Begin IB

Start IB coursework and register for any SL exams. Full diploma students begin CAS, Theory of Knowledge, and possibly the Extended Essay.

12th

Finish and test

Complete senior-year HL and SL courses, register for final exams, finish core requirements, and keep track of college application deadlines.

IB Student Board

Use the students who already know the program

The IB Student Board is student-run and helps coordinate support among students, teachers, parents, and the IB coordinator. They help students in 9th and 10th grade map out schedules and understand IB expectations before starting the program.

This organization also helps with CAS opportunities and keeps students informed about requirements and events.
